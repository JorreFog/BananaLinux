New in 0.5.0

Faster, cooler and wireless.

Tested by the QA team on both handhelds through twelve beta builds.

  • BananaDS, 12.6% faster. It runs 5 °C cooler, and a full-speed game needs about a quarter less CPU.
  • 3x and 4x resolution. Experimental, in BananaDS. 4x is the RG DS Plus's native screen size.
  • Low latency, fixed. Experimental: in BananaDS a press shows about two refreshes sooner.
  • Bluetooth. Headphones and speakers with aptX, aptX HD, LDAC and FastStream, and controllers.
  • Updates over Wi-Fi. Checked, downloaded and verified on the handheld.
  • Your library on a home server. Games, box art and saves kept in step with Romhost or RomM.
  • Companions on the second screen. A redesigned Pokémon companion, and game companions for 500+ GBA games.
  • Ten layout themes, built in. Made with the Banana Theme Studio; pick one in Settings.
  • A new first boot. An arrival animation, then a step-by-step setup.

Features

A small bunch of thoughtful features.

Built around two screens, a clamshell and the games you want to play.

Two ways to play DSChoose SuperDrastic or BananaDS, with each DS screen on its own display. F + SELECT swaps them.
Close the lidTake a break. Your game pauses when the handheld sleeps and resumes when you open it. After a long sleep on the battery it saves and powers off.
Beyond Nintendo DSPlay GBA, Game Boy, NES, SNES, Mega Drive, PlayStation and N64 through RetroArch. In 0.5.0 N64 is up to twice as fast, and PlayStation games no longer stutter or crackle.
Updates over Wi-FiSettings finds, downloads and checks the update. It installs to the other slot, so the previous version stays one restart away.
BluetoothHeadphones, speakers and controllers. Wi-Fi keeps working while Bluetooth plays.
Connected when you wantCopy games over Wi-Fi, sync with a home server, and use BananaDS for RetroAchievements and online DS play.
CompanionsThe second screen shows your Pokémon party and battles, or status, items and maps for 500+ GBA games.
English and Simplified ChineseChoose your language in Settings. Simplified Chinese translation by Majirefy; some newer text remains in English.

Nintendo DS

Two emulators. Your choice.

Both put each DS screen on its own display and include an in-game menu for save states, shaders and quick settings. Choose a default in Settings > Nintendo DS, or press X in the library to choose one for a game.

DraStic, built for both screens

DraStic by Exophase, adapted for the RG DS and RG DS Plus.

  • 2x rendering with Gengis Engine 3D
  • Per-game CPU tuning
  • Save slots with both screens as their picture
  • Low latency (experimental)

melonDS, with more ways to play

The melonDS core on BananaOS's display path and menu.

  • 12.6% faster in 0.5.0, and 5 °C cooler
  • 3x and 4x resolution (experimental)
  • RetroAchievements and Nintendo WFC online play
  • Real microphone, cheats and fast-forward

Layout themes

Make the menu yours.

In 0.5.0 a theme file can draw the whole menu: where everything is, how it looks and how it moves. Ten layout themes come built in: pick one in Settings > Display & sound > Theme. To make your own, or edit one of these, use the Banana Theme Studio, the theme editor that runs in your browser.

Under the hood

A small system, built around your handheld.

Built with Buildroot, BananaOS combines a Linux kernel, BusyBox init and a dedicated two-screen interface. No desktop or compositor required.

  • System and files, kept separate. The system lives in a read-only SquashFS image. Games, saves and settings live on an exFAT partition you can access from your computer.
  • Straight to the menu. The interface starts as soon as the data partition is mounted.
  • Room for the game. Before launching a game, the menu releases the screens, controls and audio for the emulator to use.
  • Two system slots. Updates come over Wi-Fi and install to the other slot, keeping the previous system version available.

Your next game starts here.

Choose the image for your handheld, flash a microSD card and boot BananaOS.

Download for RG DS Download for RG DS Plus

Beta 0.5.0. Read the known issues before installing. Flashing erases the selected card, so back up anything you want to keep.

  1. Flash your card. Use the BananaLinux Flasher by jesussaves-ai, or open the matching .img.gz image in balenaEtcher without unzipping it. Check the selected drive before you start.
  2. Start your handheld. Insert the card and power on. The first boot prepares your data partition, then guides you through setup step by step: language, Wi-Fi, time zone, your DS's name, RetroAchievements and box art. Skip any step with B.
  3. Add your games. Open the BANANA drive on your computer and copy games into roms/nds, roms/gba and the other system folders. You can also copy them over Wi-Fi.

Already using BananaOS? From 0.4.x, put banana-rgds-plus-0.5.0.update.tar (RG DS Plus) or banana-rgds-0.5.0.update.tar (RG DS) in banana/updates on the card and install it from Settings > System > Update. Games, saves and settings stay. From 0.5.0 on, updates come over Wi-Fi. More in the update guide.
