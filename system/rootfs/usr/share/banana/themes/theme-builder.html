🍌 Banana Theme Builder

Pick colours and files, watch the menu change, then download theme.json and copy it (with its pictures, fonts and sounds) to banana/themes on the card. Choose it in Settings > Display & sound > Theme.

The theme

The selection's colour and its four shades: light (text on it), dark (its shadow), band (its top band), low (its bottom edge).

Colours

A colour you don't change keeps the base palette's (shown greyed). The slider is opacity: right is solid. ↺ puts a colour back.

Pictures, fonts, logo

File names are relative to the .json's folder. Wallpapers: PNG or JPEG, 1024 × 768 for the RG DS Plus, 640 × 480 for the RG DS (others are scaled to fill). Fonts: TTF. Pick a file to see it in the preview: it stays on this page, only its name goes into the theme.

System icons

The system's id as in systems.cfg (nds, gba, gb, gbc, nes, snes, megadrive, psx, n64, ports, favorites, search, recently-played…) and a PNG of about 128 × 128.

Sounds

A .wav file for a menu sound, or off. Empty keeps Banana's.
